Formal ER-to-Relational Transformation Algorithms Used

Rule 1: Strong Entities PATIENT, LOCATION, AMBULANCE, HOSPITAL map directly to base relations with declared PKs.
Rule 2: 1:N FK Insertion PK of 1-side is injected as Foreign Key into N-side child (e.g. hospital_id into DEPARTMENT; dept_id into BED).
Rule 3: Ternary Associative Ternary dispatch between Emergency, Ambulance, and Hospital maps to associative table RESOURCE_ALLOCATION.
Rule 4: Trigger Audit Mapping The 1:N Logs relationship maps to EMERGENCY_STATUS_HISTORY, populated strictly by database triggers.
