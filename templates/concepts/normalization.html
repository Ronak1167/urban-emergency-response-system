1NF: Enforcing Atomic Domains & Extracting Repeating Groups Condition: All attribute values must be atomic. Solution: The multi-valued equipment array is decoupled into an independent relation: EQUIPMENT(equipment_id, ambulance_id, equipment_name, serial_no, is_functional).

2NF: Elimination of Partial Key Dependencies Condition: Must be in 1NF, and every non-prime attribute must depend on the entire candidate key (no partial dependencies on a subset of a composite key).

Surrogate Key Strategy & Full Dependency Proof

In multi-entity associations (such as dispatch matching emergency, vehicle, and hospital), candidate keys are composite: {emergency_id, ambulance_id, hospital_id}. If patient name or hospital phone were stored in this table, they would depend only on emergency_id or hospital_id (partial dependency!).

• Partial Dependency Eliminated: Patient details separated into PATIENT(patient_id, ...)
• Partial Dependency Eliminated: Hospital details separated into HOSPITAL(hospital_id, ...)
• Full Dependency Preserved: RESOURCE_ALLOCATION holds ONLY attributes of the dispatch event itself (dispatch_time, arrival_time, status).
3NF: Elimination of Transitive Dependencies Condition: Must be in 2NF, and no non-prime attribute may transitively depend on the primary key ($X \rightarrow Y$ and $Y \rightarrow Z$). Every attribute must depend strictly on the superkey alone!

3NF Solution Applied:

We extracted geographic location attributes into the normalized entity:
LOCATION(location_id [PK], address_line, zone_area, landmark, postal_code, city_district)

Now, HOSPITAL and EMERGENCY merely hold the foreign key location_id. Transitive dependencies are 100% eliminated!

Mathematical Functional Dependencies (FDs) Enforced:

FD 1: patient_id → {name, age, gender, contact, blood_group}
FD 2: ambulance_id → {vehicle_no, type, status, base_station}
FD 3: vehicle_no → ambulance_id (Candidate Key)
FD 4: equipment_id → {ambulance_id, equipment_name, serial_no, is_functional}
FD 5: {dept_id, bed_number} → {floor_no, bed_type, is_occupied}
FD 6: location_id → {address_line, zone_area, landmark, postal_code, city}
